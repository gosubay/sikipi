How to play Si Ki Pi

Singapore CNY house rules used in this game.

Split four cards into two hands

You and the banker each get four cards from a standard 52-card deck. Make two two-card hands. Put your weaker hand on top and your stronger hand below. Both hands can have the same score.

Rank each two-card hand

  1. Pairs are strongest. A-A is highest, followed by K-K down to 2-2. Suits have no effect on rank.
  2. 2–8 Gong beats any ordinary point hand. With Gong on, a 2 and an 8 beat 9 points but lose to every pair. With Gong off, they score 0.
  3. Ordinary hands score 0–9. Aces count as 1, cards 2–9 count at face value, and 10, J, Q and K count as 0. Add the two values and keep the last digit. For example, 7 + 6 scores 3 and K + 9 scores 9.

Compare hands with the banker

Compare weak against weak and strong against strong. Beat the banker on both sides to earn +1 point. Win one side and lose the other for a 0-point push. Lose both sides and score −1 point.

By default, the banker takes any tied side. If you select Round pushes, a tie on either side makes the whole round worth 0.

Example round

You have 2, 8, K, 9. With Gong on, put K + 9 (9 points) on top and 2 + 8 (Gong) below. If the banker has 7 points on top and a pair of 4s below, you win the weak side and lose the strong side. The round is a push.

This preset uses points and omits suit bonuses, Bobo and Jin Gang payouts.
